All files and their SHA-256 checksums: the latest release on GitHub.

First launch during the beta. Crastio isn't yet signed with an Apple or Microsoft certificate, so your computer asks for a confirmation the first time:
  • Mac: open the .dmg and drag Crastio to Applications. Open it once; macOS says it can't check it. Then go to System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll to "Crastio was blocked", and choose Open Anyway.
  • Windows: SmartScreen shows "Windows protected your PC". Choose More info, then Run anyway.
Only do this for a file downloaded from this page or from the GitHub release above. You can compare its checksum with the published one.

After installing

Start using Crastio right away without an account, or create one to sync between your computers and share projects with other people — during the beta, with your GitHub account (sign-up by email opens soon). Your data is in a local database on your computer; Settings › Data exports it. Crastio updates itself when a new version is out.

Uninstalling

Mac: quit Crastio and move it from Applications to the Trash. Your data stays in ~/Library/Application Support/app.crastio.desktop until you delete that folder. Windows: Settings › Apps › Crastio › Uninstall; the uninstaller asks whether to keep your data.
