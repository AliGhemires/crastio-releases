0.3.0 — first beta

  • Projects and collaboration: shared projects, Board with your own columns and tags, List, Timeline, members and roles, comments and mentions.
  • Sign in with Google or GitHub, or with a code by email.
  • Watchers (optional): Outlook, course calendars, feeds and public pages become tasks.
  • Connect an AI assistant such as Claude Code (MCP): plans wait for your approval.
  • Start dates for deadlines; scheduled and repeating activities; Today.
  • Automatic, signed updates.
  • Settings organised in panes, with search.
